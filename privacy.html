Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Classroom MCP is a personal, read-only tool. It runs only on its developer's own computer and is used only with the developer's own Google account.

Data it reads

With the user's permission it reads Google Classroom courses, course materials, assignments, announcements and topics, and downloads Google Drive files attached to them.

How the data is used

Downloaded files are saved only to the user's own computer. The data is not sent to the developer or to any other server, is not sold or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Storage and deletion

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the user's computer. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
